# Global Portfolio Sections

Phase 2 – Stage 3

Home Page is part of the main portfolio navigation. Program Learning Outcomes and Final Musings are separate sections under Level 1. All three remain outside the twelve Level 1 subject folders.

## 1. Global Navigation Structure

```text
Individual Student Portfolio
├── Home Page (main portfolio navigation)
├── Level 1
│   ├── MED 101 – Integrated Basic Sciences
│   ├── MED 102 – Family, Community, & Preventive Medicine I
│   ├── MED 103 – Medical Humanities I
│   ├── Program Learning Outcomes (separate Level 1 section)
│   └── Final Musings – Level 1 (separate Level 1 section)
└── Future academic levels (configurable)
```

The Home Page, PLOs, and Final Musings do not add to the subject-folder count. Future levels may use a different placement according to their academic requirements.

## 2. Home Page

**Source:** Original Portfolio Instructions PDF, p. 4.

| Section | Required content | Status |
|---|---|---|
| All About Me | Three-quarter portrait photograph and brief personal introduction | Confirmed by college document |
| My Learning Philosophy | Student-authored learning philosophy | Confirmed by college document |
| My Why | Student-authored reason for pursuing medicine | Confirmed by college document |
| The Good Doctor | Student-authored view of the good doctor | Confirmed by college document |

### Home Page organization

- The profile photograph and personal introduction are profile/personal content.
- My Learning Philosophy, My Why, and The Good Doctor are independent personal reflection sections.
- These sections may be referenced by MED 103 questions, but remain global content.
- No additional Home Page questions or required artifacts are added.

## 3. Program Learning Outcomes

**Source:** Original Portfolio Instructions PDF, pp. 8–9.

The global PLO section contains all eleven official outcomes. It is a separate section under Level 1. Each outcome provides a place for:

- Student-selected supporting artifact associations.
- Artifact description and significance.
- Short reflection relating evidence to the outcome.

### PLO organization rules

- One artifact may support multiple PLOs.
- Each PLO association should include its own relevance explanation and short reflection.
- No fixed subject-to-PLO relationship is invented.
- The section records evidence and does not formally declare PLO attainment.
- PLO entries are not additional subject folders.

Full PLO statements and the evidence-entry pattern are documented in [03_PLO_CONTENT_MAPPING.md](./03_PLO_CONTENT_MAPPING.md).

## 4. Final Musings – Level 1

**Source:** Original Portfolio Instructions PDF, p. 9.

Final Musings is a separate section under Level 1 containing five prescribed essay questions:

1. Has your learning philosophy changed?
2. Has your reason to pursue medicine changed?
3. What made them change? What made them remain as is?
4. How do you see your entire Level 1 journey contribute to your goal as a good doctor?
5. What was the single most important thing you learned in Level 1?

### Final Musings response organization

- Present all five questions in the prescribed order.
- Provide one student-authored essay response for each question.
- Allow the student to save and edit responses.
- Keep each response associated with Level 1 and its question.
- Supporting evidence may be referenced when the student considers it relevant, but the source does not prescribe a separate artifact requirement for Final Musings.
- Faculty may view authorized responses but cannot edit, comment, provide feedback, or grade them.

## 5. Global Evidence and Privacy Rules

Global sections follow the same evidence and integrity rules where evidence is referenced:

- Do not include patient names, faces, identifying dates, record numbers, or other patient-identifiable information.
- Obtain required permissions for clinical or community material.
- Acknowledge references, collaborators, and artificial-intelligence use.
- Keep artifact files protected and associated with the student’s private portfolio.

## 6. Placement Validation

- Home Page: main portfolio navigation, not a subject folder.
- Program Learning Outcomes: separate Level 1 section, not a subject folder.
- Final Musings: separate Level 1 section, not a subject folder.
- Level 1 subjects: exactly three.
- Subject folders: exactly four per subject, twelve total.
- Future academic-level placement: configurable according to eventual academic requirements.
