# High-Fidelity UI Design Specifications

These specifications describe the intended visual composition for implementation; they are not production code or rendered mockups.

## Authentication

Centered white form on a soft pink-tinted page, small SLRP identity mark, clear role paths, and no distracting medical stock imagery. Instructor registration adds a prominent invitation-code field and a note that portfolio access requires student authorization.

## Student Dashboard

White reading canvas with pink sidebar selection, a warm welcome, “Continue your reflection” location, privacy status, and two or three purposeful next actions. Avoid KPI grids.

## Home Page

Personal editorial layout: portrait and introduction beside or above Learning Philosophy, My Why, and The Good Doctor sections. Editing uses calm form panels and visible save state.

## Level 1 and Subject Pages

Use a subject index with three clear pink-accented subject entries and separate PLO/Final Musings entries. Subject pages show four folder sections. MED 102 includes a dedicated eight-domain index and labels the parent grouping as provisional.

## Folder and Domain Pages

Use a clear academic title, source/context summary, prompt list, artifact evidence list, and domain navigation. Do not collapse the eight MED 102 domains into four visual records.

## Reflection Editor

Two-column desktop composition: official prompt/context on the left, long-form answer on the right; single-column on mobile. Related artifacts and save state sit below the answer. Instructor guidance is not embedded into the student answer field.

## Artifact Management

Artifact library uses readable rows with type, title, academic context, date, and privacy status. Upload uses a focused step panel with file policy, progress, association controls, and recoverable errors.

## PLO Attainment

Use an eleven-item vertical index with an open PLO detail pane. Evidence selection reuses existing artifacts. Separate “what it demonstrates” and reflection fields support narrative evidence, not grading.

## Final Musings

Use a quiet reading/writing flow with one official question at a time, progress by question count, save-and-continue, and a review summary. Do not imply submission approval.

## Instructor Dashboard and Portfolio

Use a restrained authorized-portfolio list, then a read-only portfolio viewer with consistent student academic navigation. Pink highlights current context; feedback appears as a separate guidance layer.

## Client Administrator Invitation Settings

Use a high-clarity operational page with current status, expiry, reveal/copy, replace, and disable actions. Destructive actions use explicit warnings: existing instructor accounts and portfolio grants are unaffected.
