# Interaction and Animation Guidelines

## Principles

Motion is purposeful, brief, and optional. It should clarify navigation, feedback, and state changes without making the portfolio feel playful or distracting.

## Approved Motion Patterns

- Page content fade/translate: 120–180ms on route transition.
- Sidebar and mobile drawer: 180–240ms ease-out.
- Accordion/domain expansion: 160–220ms.
- Save success: subtle status icon/check transition, under 250ms.
- Upload progress: continuous progress indicator; no decorative animation.
- Dialog entry/exit: opacity plus small scale, 160–200ms.

## Prohibited or Avoided Motion

No auto-rotating carousels, bouncing controls, parallax, excessive confetti, animated medical imagery, or motion that obscures a reflection prompt.

## Accessibility

Respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not use motion as the sole indication of completion, error, authorization, or upload state. Maintain focus when dialogs open/close and when drawers change.
