# Final UI/UX Design Specifications

## Student

Use an academic sidebar and breadcrumb context. The dashboard is a calm portfolio entry point with privacy status and a small number of meaningful next actions. Reflection pages prioritize official prompts and long-form reading. Artifact pages support secure upload, metadata, associations, progress, and recovery. Instructor access clearly lists active/revoked grants.

## Instructor

Use an authorized-portfolio shell with no public directory. Student content is read-only and has no edit, delete, upload, or replacement controls. Reviews and feedback are separate, attributed, and clearly labeled as instructor guidance.

## Client Administrator

Use a narrow operational shell for the shared invitation code. Replacement and disablement require confirmation and explain that existing accounts and portfolio authorizations are unchanged.

## States

All roles require loading, empty, validation, success, failure, unauthorized, revoked-access, session-expired, and unsaved-change states. Upload screens require progress, cancellation/retry, and file-policy errors. Unauthorized states must not reveal private portfolio existence.

## Visual and Responsive Requirements

Use the approved 60% white, 30% `#FF669B`, and 10% `#F9E7A2` foundation. Support desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts; preserve academic context, prompt readability, privacy status, and primary actions at every width.
