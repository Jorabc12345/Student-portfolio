# Phase 4 Summary

Phase 4 database requirements, conceptual modeling, logical schema design, and validation are complete as documentation.

The final design contains 35 proposed tables, including a client-controlled shared instructor invitation code, configurable artifact policy settings, and optional feedback revision, completion, and progress structures. It supports individual student ownership, student-authorized instructor access, revocation, verified instructor roles, reviews, feedback, private artifacts with short-lived signed URLs, eight separately accessible MED 102 domains, and future academic levels.

The design is ready for Phase 5 planning but not for production migration. Remaining work includes the exact verification process, artifact limits and retention, feedback edit/delete rules, backup ownership, and implementation/testing of RLS and Storage policies.
