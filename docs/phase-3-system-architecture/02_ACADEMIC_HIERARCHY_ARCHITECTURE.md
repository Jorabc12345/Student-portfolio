# Academic Hierarchy Architecture

## Phase 3 – Stage 2

## 1. Hierarchy

```text
Portfolio
├── Home Page
└── Level
    ├── Subject
    │   └── Main Folder
    │       └── Learning Domain or Content
    │           ├── Reflections
    │           └── Artifacts
    ├── PLO Attainment
    └── Final Musings
```

Levels and their contents are configurable. Version 1 populates only Level 1.

## 2. Level 1 Structure

| Subject | Main folders | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| MED 101 – Integrated Basic Sciences | 4 | Confirmed |
| MED 102 – Family, Community, & Preventive Medicine I | 4 | Confirmed count; parent grouping provisional |
| MED 103 – Medical Humanities I | 4 | Confirmed count; period/folder naming remains subject to Phase 2 decisions |

There are exactly twelve main subject folders. Home Page, PLO Attainment, and Final Musings are separate Level 1 sections and are not included in that count.

## 3. Stable Folder IDs

### MED 101

- `MED101-F01` – 1st Bimonthly – Medical Foundations
- `MED101-F02` – 2nd Bimonthly – Knowledge Development
- `MED101-F03` – 3rd Bimonthly – Learning Integration
- `MED101-F04` – 4th Bimonthly – Year-End Reflection

Each period is a main folder containing CERAE, Reflections, and Artifacts.

### MED 102

- `MED102-F01` – Patient & Family-Centered Care (proposed)
- `MED102-F02` – Health & Social Determinants (proposed)
- `MED102-F03` – Healthcare Systems & Primary Care (proposed)
- `MED102-F04` – Community & Professional Development (proposed)

The eight domains remain separate:

1. Patient-centered care.
2. Family-focused care.
3. Social determinants of health.
4. Global and national health frameworks.
5. Primary health care and Universal Health Care.
6. Systems thinking.
7. Community engagement and social accountability.
8. Professional identity formation.

The parent-folder assignments above are not finalized. A domain must retain its own identity even if its parent changes. Evidence may link to multiple domains.

### MED 103

- `MED103-F01` – Learning Identity & Humanistic Foundations
- `MED103-F02` – Patient Relationships & Cultural Understanding
- `MED103-F03` – Professional Identity, Ethics & Well-Being
- `MED103-F04` – Ethical Leadership & Social Responsibility

These are configurable display values aligned with the four official themed periods; official source themes and questions remain authoritative.

## 4. Global Sections

- Home Page: main portfolio navigation.
- PLO Attainment: separate Level 1 section containing eleven PLOs.
- Final Musings: separate Level 1 section containing five prescribed questions.

These sections are not subject folders. Future levels may use different section placement.

## 5. Content Relationships

- A reflection belongs to a portfolio and an academic context.
- An artifact belongs to a portfolio and stores one binary.
- Artifact-domain and artifact-PLO relationships are many-to-many.
- A reflection may reference one or more artifacts.
- One artifact may support multiple PLOs with separate relevance explanations.
- PLO association does not automatically declare attainment.

## 6. Academic Integrity

Exact source prompts must be preserved. The architecture must support artifact descriptions, permissions, references, collaborators, and artificial-intelligence-use acknowledgements without requiring patient-identifiable information.
